ABOUT DR. AMOGH

General Medicine, done carefully

Dr. Amogh G is a General Medicine physician at Pragati Hospital, Mandya. He completed his MBBS and later pursued MD in General Medicine, building his clinical career around the assessment and management of a broad range of medical concerns.

General Medicine looks at the bigger picture of your health — from everyday symptoms to ongoing health concerns that need a closer look. Dr. Amogh takes time to understand the patient's concerns, medical history, and the changes they've been noticing before deciding what a problem needs.

FROM DR. AMOGH

Simple answers to common health questions.

Energy & fatigue

Why am I always tired?

Sleep, stress, and everyday habits explain most of it. But fatigue that lasts for weeks, or comes with other changes, is worth getting checked rather than pushed through.

Headaches

Is my headache normal?

Most headaches are ordinary and pass on their own. A sudden, severe headache, or one that's different from your usual pattern, is a reason to get seen sooner rather than later.

Check-ups

What happens at a check-up?

A conversation about how you've been feeling, a physical examination, and any tests that make sense based on your age and history — nothing more than that's needed.
